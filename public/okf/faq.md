## Getting started

Where is Renegade Reformer?

Renegade Reformer is at 22a Church Road, Redfield, Bristol BS5 9JA, a two minute walk from Lawrence Hill train station. You can reach us any time at studio@renegadereformer.co.uk.

How do I contact the studio?

You can email us at studio@renegadereformer.co.uk or message us on WhatsApp at +44 7846 849456. The WhatsApp number is for messages only, it does not take voice calls, and it is usually the quickest way to reach us.

How long is a class and how many people are in it?

Every session is 50 minutes. Every class is capped small, so you always get proper attention from your instructor and space to work at your own level.

What should I wear?

Fitted, comfortable workout clothes: leggings, shorts and a fitted top are ideal. Avoid anything baggy, and avoid zips or buttons that could damage the equipment. Grip socks are required for every reformer class; we sell them at the studio if you don't have a pair.

## Booking & classes

How do I book a class?

All classes are booked through Momence, whether you're using a drop-in, a class pack or a membership.

Do I need a membership, or can I just drop in?

Drop-ins, class packs and monthly memberships are all available, see full pricing.

How do I check in when I arrive?

Check yourself in on the iPad at the front desk. This confirms your attendance and protects you from an accidental no-show fee, which matters especially if you arrive close to your class start time. If you arrive late and forget to check in, let your instructor know before the end of the day and we'll check you in after class.

How do I get into the studio?

For safety and security, the studio door stays locked and access is managed through an automated door system. Your mobile pass unlocks the door directly from the Momence app, but it's only valid from 15 minutes before your class starts until the class begins. You can't enter earlier or later than that window.

What if I'm running late?

Late entry may not be permitted once class has started, for safety reasons. If you arrive after your access pass has expired, or are refused entry due to late arrival, your booking credits may still be deducted.

Can I join a waitlist for a full class?

Yes. Only join if you can genuinely attend, though. If a space opens up you may be added automatically, and normal cancellation and no-show rules still apply.

## Cancellations & no-shows

What's your cancellation policy?

We operate a 24-hour cancellation policy for every class: drop-in, class pack or membership.

Cancel more than 24 hours before your class and your credit or booking is returned to your account automatically. Cancel within 24 hours and it's a late cancellation, the credit is lost. Cancel within 90 minutes of the class and a **£5 late cancellation fee** also applies.

To cancel, log in to your account on Momence and select "cancel yourself" on the booking.

What happens if I don't show up to a class I've booked?

A **£10 no-show fee** applies. Always check in on the front desk iPad when you arrive to avoid this being applied by mistake.

Why do you charge cancellation and no-show fees?

It keeps classes running properly and lets us offer your spot to someone on the waitlist. As a small studio, we can't run with sudden half-empty classes. Even cancelling within the 24-hour window still helps another member take your place. We appreciate your cooperation.

## Memberships

How much does reformer Pilates cost at Renegade Reformer?

Drop-in classes are £25. Class packs are £95 for 4 classes, £175 for 8 classes and £252 for 12 classes, each valid for 3 months from first use.

Monthly memberships are Core at £85 a month for 4 classes (£21.25 per class), Pro at £150 a month for 8 classes (£18.75 per class) and Elite at £195 a month for 12 classes (£16.25 per class).

Full details are on our pricing page.

How do membership credits work?

Every reformer class costs 3 credits. Depending on your plan, you get 12, 24 or 36 credits a month (equivalent to 4, 8 or 12 reformer classes). Credits renew on your billing date, don't roll over if unused, and don't cover Yoga, Barre or Mat Pilates.

How do I pay, and does my membership renew automatically?

Membership fees are paid monthly in advance by card, direct debit or another approved method, and renew automatically each month on your billing date until you cancel. If a payment fails, access to bookings and remaining credits may be temporarily suspended until it's resolved.

How do I cancel my membership?

Cancel through your Momence account or by emailing studio@renegadereformer.co.uk, giving at least 14 days' notice before your next billing date. Verbal requests, Instagram DMs or messages to instructors don't count as formal notice. If we get less than 14 days' notice, your next payment may still be taken and the membership will end the following billing cycle.

Can I pause my membership?

Membership freezes are not offered as standard. In exceptional circumstances, such as illness, injury or extended travel, Renegade Reformer may agree to pause a membership at its discretion.

Requests must be made at least 7 days in advance by emailing studio@renegadereformer.co.uk and may require supporting information.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, changes usually take effect from your next billing cycle unless we agree otherwise.

Can someone else use my membership?

No. Memberships, credits and bookings are personal to the named member and can't be shared, transferred, resold or used by anyone else, including friends or family. This is for safety, insurance and health-screening reasons.

## Health, safety & eligibility

Do I need to tell you about injuries or health conditions?

Yes, before class. Let your instructor know if you're injured, recovering from surgery, have a medical condition, or have experienced any change in your health, so exercises can be adapted around it.

Is reformer Pilates safe during pregnancy?

Yes, with the right support. Pregnant and postnatal clients can only attend sessions led by an instructor qualified in pre and postnatal exercise, so let us know in advance so we can place you in the right class and support you safely.

What's the minimum age to join?

14. Anyone between 14 and 18 needs a parent or legal guardian to book on their behalf and complete a parental consent declaration, and must attend an appropriate beginner or Foundations class unless we've agreed otherwise.

Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes. Booking or attending a class confirms you've read and agreed to our health declaration and studio waiver, which covers things like informing us of any medical conditions, following instructor guidance, and stopping if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.
