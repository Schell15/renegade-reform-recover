# Foundations, Renegade and Rebuild — three ways in, one Renegade approach to *strength and control*.

Movement at Renegade is motivated by music, where tradition is met with progression.
We elevate pilates as a source of **personal power** — one that makes
you feel bold and speaks to the inner desire to use movement as a release.

Renegade is an **immersive pilates experience**. We introduce a new concept
to Bristol, fusing pilates, fitness, set design and world-class audio and lighting
production to guide your flow — designed and built by some of Bristol's finest
creative minds. **The first of its kind in the UK.**
